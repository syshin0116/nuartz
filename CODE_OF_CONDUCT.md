# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nuartz welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact information on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may remove content, close contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
